    Platform readiness

    There is no single size that satisfies everyone. Apple's artist page asks for 4000px while CD Baby and TuneCore reject anything over 3000px, so the practical answer is to master at 4000 and deliver a 3000px square JPEG in sRGB, under 10 MB. Beatport also rejects art tagged above 72 DPI, and SoundCloud's own upload form caps images at 2 MB, so keep a compressed copy for that. Specs were checked against platform documentation in August 2026 and do drift, so confirm against your own distributor before release.

    The minimums above are what each platform publishes. The coverforge command-line tool is deliberately stricter for some of them, because it refuses to render a target from a master too small to fill it honestly. If the two disagree about a platform, that is why, and the reason is written next to each target in coverforge/targets.toml.
