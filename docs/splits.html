Write down who owns what

Two different things get split on every track, and people forget that. The composition is what GEMA and other societies pay on. The recording is what GVL and your distributor pay on. Fill both in, print it, get it signed while everyone is still friendly.

This builds a document. It is not legal advice, and it is not registration: registering with a society is a separate step you still have to do.

This is the specific recording. In Germany the neighbouring rights side is GVL, where performers and the recording producer (often the label) collect from two different pots. Master ownership is usually the label unless you agreed otherwise, so write down what you actually agreed.
